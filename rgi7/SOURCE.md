# RGI7 / GTN-G glaciated-region boundaries (HKH)

`hkh_o1regions.geojson` is the three RGI 7.0 first-order (O1) region
polygons that make up the Hindu Kush Himalaya glaciated extent -- Central
Asia (13), South Asia West (14), South Asia East (15) -- extracted from
the official GTN-G region-boundary product (RGI7 defines its regions
against the GTN-G glacier-region standard, not its own separate
boundary).

Source: GTN-G (2023), *GTN-G Glacier Regions*. Global Terrestrial Network
for Glaciers. DOI: 10.5904/gtng-glacreg-2023-07.
https://www.gtn-g.ch/data_catalogue_glacreg/ -> `GlacReg_2023.zip`
(`GTN-G_202307_o1regions.shp`), downloaded 2026-09-29. Open access for
scientific/educational use with citation.

This is the *region boundary* product -- one polygon per region showing
the overall glaciated extent used to scope RGI7 -- not individual
glacier/complex outlines, which is a much larger, separate RGI7 product.
